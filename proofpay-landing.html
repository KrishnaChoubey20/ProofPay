Three ways a vault can pay

Pick the rhythm, not just the rate.

Every company gets its own isolated vault, deployed by the factory on demand. What differs is how money leaves it.

Scheduled

Lock it, release it on a date.

Set aside a lump sum that unlocks at a specific time. Built for contractor milestones, sign-on bonuses and cliff vesting, where the whole point is that nothing moves early.

Streaming

Pay out linearly, second by second.

A continuous rate accrues in the vault and a worker can claim whatever has vested at any moment — no waiting on a monthly cycle for money already earned.

Batch

One run, a whole team claims.

Load a multi-worker payroll in a single transaction. Every teammate claims their own allocation with their own wallet — the employer never touches anyone's keys.

Built for teams that don't share a border

The parts that make it trustworthy.

FACTORY

Dynamic vault factory

Each company's vault is deployed on demand from a Soroban factory contract — isolated funds, never a shared pool.

CUSTODY

Non-interactive claims

Workers claim with their own wallet signature. The employer funds the vault and never sees a private key.

ASSETS

Multi-asset vaults

Fund a vault in XLM or USDC and let workers claim in whichever asset the vault was set up to hold.

PRIVACY

Selective income proofs

Generate a cryptographic proof of a pay history for a landlord or visa office — without exposing every transaction.

The paperwork, without the paperwork

One statement, verifiable on-chain.

Instead of exporting a bank statement, a worker generates a signed certificate straight from their claim history — a landlord or embassy can check it against the ledger in seconds.

  • Chooses a date range and an asset to summarize.
  • ProofPay reads only confirmed claims from that worker's vault.
  • The certificate carries a hash that anyone can verify against the chain, without ever pulling the raw transaction history.
